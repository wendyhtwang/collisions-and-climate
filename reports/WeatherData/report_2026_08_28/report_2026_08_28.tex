\subsection*{Known properties of the provisional definition}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Look-ahead: each county's reference distribution uses the full 1982â2025 sample, so later winters inform how earlier winters are classified.
\item Detrending removes each county's linear trend, so gradual warming is excluded from the shock measure by construction.
\item A county's mapped frequency partly reflects the skewness and tail shape of its residual distribution, not exposure to warm shocks alone.
\item The 1.5 SD cut-off is a convention, not an estimated threshold.
\item For regression use, compare against a fixed-baseline alternative (e.g. above the county's 1982â2000 mean by 1.5 baseline SD) or use the continuous standardised anomaly.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Current choice.} Detrended county residual > 1.5 SD of the full 1982â2025 sample; minimum 30 winters\par
\textbf{Issue.} Uses future winters to classify earlier ones and removes secular warming by construction; threshold is a convention. Alternatives: fixed 1982â2000 baseline, or the continuous standardised anomaly.\par

\textbf{Issue.} Estimand is the average county, not an area-, population- or road-weighted national average. A VMT- or road-mile-weighted series may be the relevant exposure weight for the collisions stage.\par
